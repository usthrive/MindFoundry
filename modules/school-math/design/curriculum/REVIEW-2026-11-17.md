## Verdict: **READY AFTER FIXES**

The mathematics is almost all right (231 values recomputed, 1 wrong-answer-rule pair in a Grade 2 check and 6 more across the other grades; no wrong keyed answer anywhere). The design is coherent and unusually careful about who does the thinking. But four gate-level problems must be fixed before the parent sees it: the school test's own numbers appear in the curriculum (240, 348, 27, 15, 738 — one of them in a sentence the teacher says aloud), the entry plan can skip the one lesson that repairs an exam skill, the method-ledger gate misses five skills the Best Brains-style module teaches, and two input surfaces as specified cannot grade what they ask (the open-number-line tool does the mental arithmetic for the child; the typed shape hint prints the answer).

Counts: **BLOCKER 5 · FIX 17 · ADVISORY 14.**
