## The rules that make the year fit

- **A school year = 36 weeks of 5 short school days (about 10–15 minutes, N questions, DD1).**
  Weekends and school breaks are off by default; a parent may add light days.
- **Days per lesson (planning numbers):** 2 days for a number, operation or bar-model lesson (a
  lesson day, then a practice day: "knows" needs two separate days, tutoring-app-design §2);
  1.5 days for a measurement, time, money, data or shape lesson; 1 day for each chapter check;
  +1 for a stretch item.
- **The year's shape:** week 1 = parent intake, a 2-day review of the last finished chapter and
  the entry check (DD24); week 18 = mid-year review; weeks 35–36 = year review and an
  end-of-grade mixed check. Units fill weeks 2–34.
- **Records set the real pace, inside the calendar.** A skill right on the first try on two
  separate days is "known" and its lesson ends early; the saved days become buffer. If the child
  falls more than a week behind the calendar, measurement/data/shape lessons drop to 1 day each
  and preview items pause; number, operation and bar-model lessons are never cut.
- **The parent sees one line:** "On track to finish Grade N by <date>" or "about N days behind:
  here is what we shortened."
