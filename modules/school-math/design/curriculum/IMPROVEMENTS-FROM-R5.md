## What is not proposed, and why

- **Switching the base-ten object to place-value discs in Grade 2** (Dimensions does, R5:F9): the
  school's book uses blocks until Grade 4 and DD18 matches the school's object. No change.
- **Moving G2-U03 before G2-U02** (Oak orders "additive relationships" before column addition,
  R5:F24): the first learner's entry plan (GRADE-2 §0) already runs U02 L1 first and the probe
  routes him; the sort step in proposal 1 brings the useful part forward without reordering.
- **Using MLC, Polypad, Didax or MOE simulations inside the app** (R5:F40–F42): licences forbid
  derivatives or commercial use (MLC; CC BY-NC-SA), and DD7's guided builder is already the
  module's own design. They remain teacher-side references only.
- **Timed fact drills** (Kheng Cheng deck's "set a time limit", R5:F30 source): DD13 keeps drill
  with the Kumon-style supplier.
