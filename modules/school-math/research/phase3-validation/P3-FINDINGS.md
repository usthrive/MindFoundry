## G2 ★1 — How Math in Focus 2020 Grades 1–3 lessons present and hand over models

**Searches tried.** EdReports Math in Focus (2021 listing = 2020 print edition) Gateway 1 pages for
Grades 1, 2 and 3, re-read for lesson verbs (vector 2); Nutley NJ Grade 1 and Grade 3 curriculum
guides at the known path pattern (vector 5); the 2026 Singapore school model-drawing parent deck
re-read with a PDF text tool (CR-15). Not run: the BoardDocs and sites.google.com vectors (budget,
and the two sources below already agree on the Grade 3 drawing step); the HMH S3 bucket vector
(R3 already read the Program Guide, Foundations paper and Components brochure there).

**Findings.**
1. **Grade 1 uses no bar models.** EdReports Grade 1 names connecting cubes (5.1), a counting tape
   (5.2), cubes on a place-value chart (10.3) and a Performance Task drawing ten-rods and unit cubes
   on place-value charts; no bar model, number line or ten frame. Nutley's Grade 1 guide: number
   bonds, "addition stories from pictures", place-value charts; no bar models.
   *Confirmed · High* —
   https://edreports.org/reports/detail/math-in-focus-singapore-math-2021/first-grade/gateway-one ;
   https://nutleyschools.org/sites/default/files/curriculum/NPS_Curr_Math%2CG1%2CAug2024_.pdf (E246)
2. **Grade 2 supplies the model in lessons.** EdReports Grade 2 cites the Ch 4 Key Learning
   Objectives (TE p. 181A, problems represented "using bar models") and Learning Continuum
   (pp. 181C–181E, bar models for two-step addition and subtraction), and a lesson item, Section 10.5
   Try, Problem 1, that tells pupils "Use the bar model to help you" (the model is given). Tests:
   the Ch 4 test gives a blank bar model and blank equation; the Ch 6 assessment says "Draw a bar
   model to help you solve the problem." *Confirmed · High* —
   https://edreports.org/reports/detail/math-in-focus-singapore-math-2021/second-grade/gateway-one (E243)
3. **Grade 3 asks the pupil to draw in lessons.** EdReports Grade 3: Section 7.3 Learn uses bar
   models for a fraction of a set; Section 7.3 Try, Problem 4 directs "Draw a bar model to help you."
   Nutley's Grade 3 guide teaches Ch 6 "Using Bar Models: The Four Operations" in January with the
   objective "Draw bar models to solve one and two-step" problems. Two independent sources agree
   that drawing is a lesson expectation by Grade 3. *Confirmed · High* —
   https://edreports.org/reports/detail/math-in-focus-singapore-math-2021/third-grade/gateway-one ;
   https://nutleyschools.org/sites/default/files/curriculum/NPS_Curr_Math%2CG3%2CAug2024_.pdf (E244, E245)
4. **A Singapore P2 class asks the child to choose and draw the model, with prompts.** The 2026
   school deck (now readable) works P2 examples with a part-whole model (addition and subtraction
   within 1,000) and a comparison model (length); the teacher prompts ask which model to draw and
   "Where will you put the values in the model?"; model types are cited to the ministry's 2009
   monograph, pp. 15 and 76. *Confirmed (that school) · High* —
   https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/640/f55db41b-8728-44fa-82a0-7136341ff717/Making%20Sense%20of%20Math_%20Model%20Drawing%20for%20Young%20Learners_Mathematics_Parents%20Workshop%202026.pdf (E248)
5. **The handover ladder across Grades 1–4 (synthesis).** Grade 1: no bars (pictures, cubes,
   bonds) → Grade 2: bars supplied in lessons, labelled with a "?" (one district, E62), completed on
   the Ch 4 test, drawn on the Ch 6 test → Grade 3: drawn by the pupil in lesson Try items →
   Grade 4: pupils "usually given" the model again for harder problems (EdReports Grade 4 Gateway 2,
   E61). *Inferred · Medium-High* (E247)

**Still open.** Whether Grade 2 lessons ever ask the child to *label* or *size* bars himself (the
"label" rung rests on one district, E62); what the Teacher Edition's "Concrete-Pictorial-Abstract
Progression" pages say (teacher-only, not public). **Verdict: PARTLY CLOSED** — the grade-level
handover is settled; the within-Grade-2 rung (label vs size) stays a design default (G8).

## G3 ★2 — Bar-model type sequence by grade (current US editions)

**Searches tried.** PM 2022 strand scope `PM22_Scope.pdf` and PM 2022 tables of contents
`PM22_Scope_Overviews.pdf` (vector 1, both read in full with a PDF text tool); PMCC scope; the
Dimensions Math PK–5 scope `DMPK5_Scope.pdf` (in place of the ten per-book overviews, vector 2);
EdReports Math in Focus Grades 1, 3 and 5 Gateway 1 (vector 3); the 2026 Singapore deck (vector 5
alternative; the moe.edu.sg briefing paths are 404); a peer-reviewed paper found by search
(Lee et al. 2013). Not run: the book-TOC vector (Yeap, Beckmann 6th ed., 2009 monograph) — the two
publisher sources made it unnecessary.

**Findings.**
1. **PM 2022 chapter titles (publisher TOC):** Grade 2 Ch 4 "Addition and Subtraction Using Bar
   Models" = 4A Sum and Difference · 4B Part-Whole Model · 4C Comparison Model · 4D Word Problems;
   Grade 4 Ch 4 "The Four Operations Using Bar Models"; Grade 5 Ch 5 and Ch 8 are word-problem
   chapters on fractions and decimals; no bar-model chapter in Grade 1 or Grade 3.
   *Confirmed (titles) · High* —
   https://singapore-math.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/scopes/PM22_Scope_Overviews.pdf (E249)
2. **PM 2022 scope lines by grade:** Grade 2 — solve multi-digit addition and subtraction "by using
   a bar model" (Ch 4); bar models show "relationships between numbers and unknowns" (Ch 4).
   Grade 3 — addition and subtraction with greater numbers by bar model (Ch 2); bar models for
   multiplication and division (Ch 3–4). Grade 4 — addition and subtraction with a bar model (Ch 2);
   Ch 4 four operations. Grade 5 — letters as variables for unknowns. Grade 6 — ratio bar models
   and "part/whole, comparison, and before and after bar models" for multistep problems. No bar-model
   line in the Grade 1 column. *Confirmed · High* (grade column read from page position: Medium-High) —
   https://singapore-math.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/scopes/PM22_Scope.pdf (E250)
3. **Math in Focus matches:** no bars in Grade 1 (E246); Grade 2 Ch 4 part-whole → adding on /
   taking away → comparison → two-step (E60); Grade 3 Ch 6 "Using Bar Models: The Four Operations"
   and 7.3 bar models for a fraction of a set (E244, E245); Grade 4 bar models in 1.6, 2.4, 2.7, 3.9
   (E119). EdReports Grade 5 names no bar model; it cites place-value chips (1.4) and fraction bars
   and pie diagrams (2.1); Grade 5 tests use "Show your work" constructed response, multiple select
   and multiple choice. *Confirmed · High* —
   https://edreports.org/reports/detail/math-in-focus-singapore-math-2021/fifth-grade/gateway-one (E269;
   also feeds G14 and G18)
4. **Peer-reviewed placement:** Singapore children meet algebraic word problems "in the latter part
   of Primary 4"; three model types are "commonly taught": part-whole, comparison,
   multiplication-and-division. *Confirmed · High* — Lee, Khng, Ng & Kong (2013), *Frontline
   Learning Research* 1(1), doi 10.14786/flr.v1i1.49,
   https://frontlinelearningresearch.org//index.php/journal/article/download/49/122 (E255; same
   paper as E64)
5. **Sequence (synthesis, two current series agree for Grades 1–4):** Grade 1 none (picture
   stories; PMCC 2A opens by reviewing part-whole and comparison word problems) → Grade 2
   part-whole then comparison, one- and two-step +/− → Grade 3 bars for larger +/−, × ÷ (equal
   units) and fraction of a set → Grade 4 four operations with bars, before-after heuristic, algebra
   type late in the year → Grade 5 fraction and decimal word problems, letters for unknowns →
   Grade 6 before-after and ratio bars. *Inferred · Medium-High* (E251)

**Changes the earlier picture.** E57's "P1–P2 part-whole and comparison" overstates P1: neither
current US series nor the Singapore P2 deck shows bars before Grade 2 / P2 (label annotated in the
final ledger). **Verdict: CLOSED** for Grades 1–4 (two independent current series); residual:
Math in Focus Grade 5 model sections are not named publicly.

## G4 ★3 — Sum-and-difference (Q7 type): Grade 2, or Grades 3–4?

**Searches tried.** PM 2022 TOC (vector 1, lesson titles); PMCC scope (vector 5, Grade 3 Unit 2
lesson list); Dimensions PK–5 scope; EdReports Math in Focus Grade 2 Gateway 1 (vector 3);
web search `"sum and difference" model method Primary 3 OR Primary 4 Singapore bar model two
unknowns`; web search `"P3" OR "Primary 3" math "sum and difference" ... Singapore`. Not run:
vector 2 (no EdReports review of PM 2022 surfaced in searches), vector 4 (singaporemath.com pages)
— stopped once two independent sources agreed.

**Findings.**
1. **"Sum and Difference" in the PM lineage is a concept lesson, not the two-unknowns type.**
   PMCC Grade 3 Unit 2 Lesson 3 "Sum and Difference" is four days: sum and difference · parts and
   whole · more than and less than (two days), followed by Lesson 4 one-step and two-step word
   problems. *Confirmed (lesson list) · High; content Inferred · Medium-High* —
   https://singapore-math.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/scopes/PMCC_Scope.pdf (E252)
2. Dimensions Math 3A Ch 2 has a "Sum and Difference" lesson placed before "Word Problems — Part 1/2"
   and "2-Step Word Problems". *Confirmed (titles) · High* —
   https://singapore-math.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/scopes/DMPK5_Scope.pdf (E253)
3. PM 2022 2A puts 4A Sum and Difference **before** 4B Part-Whole Model and 4C Comparison Model,
   the same pattern, so 4A is most likely the meaning-of-terms lesson. *Inferred · Medium* (E254;
   E31's Speculative label revised)
4. **Two independent sources place the two-unknowns type in Grade 4:** Beckmann's reading of
   Primary Mathematics (algebra story problems from Grade 4, E58) and Lee et al. 2013 (algebraic
   word problems in late Primary 4, E255). *Confirmed (each source) · High*
5. EdReports Math in Focus Grade 2 Gateway 1 names no sum-and-difference or two-unknowns item.
   *Confirmed (absence on that page) · High* (part of E243)
6. A Singapore school's 2022 P3/P4 parent workshop (search snippet only; the file now 404s) showed
   a comparison model with the difference marked; a third-party re-host of another P3/P4 seminar
   deck showed a sum-and-difference item (not opened: re-upload). *Speculative · Low* (E257)

**Changes the earlier picture.** CR-4's "typically Grade 3–4" rested partly on the PMCC Grade 3
lesson title; that lesson is the concept lesson, so the placement narrows to **Grade 4** (late P4)
in the Singapore lineage, about two grades above his Grade 2 test (E256, CR-16). The design
consequence in CR-4 stands and strengthens: in Grade 2 the item is stretch, taught only after
comparison bars. **Verdict: CLOSED** (residual: PM 2022 4A page content unseen, Inferred · Medium).

## G5 ★4 — Chapter-test formats, points and reporting categories, Grades 1–3

**Searches tried.** EdReports Grades 1, 2 and 3 Gateway 1 test citations (vector 1); retailer
listings for the 2020 Grade 1 and Grade 2 Assessment Guides (vector 2: Christianbook page fetched;
HMH shop and Rainbow Resource via search excerpts); web searches `"concepts, skills and
procedures" "mathematical reasoning" "statements, arguments" report`, `grade 2 math report card
"concepts, skills, and procedures" reasoning standards-based`, `Smarter Balanced mathematics claims
"Concepts and Procedures" "Communicating Reasoning"`, `"Math in Focus" 2020 assessment "concepts,
skills, and procedures"`, `"Math in Focus" chapter test "Concepts and Skills" "Problem Solving"`
(vectors 3 and 5). Not run: vector 4 (HMH Ed help pages) — budget.

**Findings.**
1. **Grade 1 formats** (EdReports): picture-supported items; multiple choice (a–d / A–D); fill-in
   blanks and number sentences; complete a pattern and describe it; draw a shape (Ch 3); tallies
   into a table (Ch 12); "circle a mistake and show correct work" (Ch 11 assessment — error
   analysis already in Grade 1); "show your work and write your answer" (Ch 7, Ch 13); Performance
   Task 10 on place-value charts. No point values. *Confirmed · High* (E258)
2. **Grade 3 formats with sections** (EdReports): Section A multiple choice (A–D), but Ch 4 Section A
   Item 12 asks to explain whether a claim is true; Section B fill-in grid, yes/no per shape, judge a
   comparison and explain; Section C explain an error, word problem with shown work; each test
   exists in paper and online versions; rubrics "for some short answer and all constructed response
   items"; no point values. *Confirmed · High* (E259)
3. **Grade 2** (EdReports re-read): the same citations as R3 (E129) and **no point values anywhere**
   on the page. *Confirmed · High* (E260)
4. **Assessment Guide sizes** (listings): Grade 1 student guide 104 pp; Grade 2 student guide
   112 pp; Grade 2 Teacher Edition 96 pp "with answer keys and recommended scoring rubrics"; a
   Chapter Test per chapter plus Cumulative Reviews. *Confirmed (listings) · Medium-High* —
   https://www.christianbook.com/math-focus-assessment-guide-grade-1/9780358103172/pd/161528 ;
   https://www.hmhco.com/shop/k12/Math-in-Focus/9780358103189 ;
   https://www.hmhco.com/shop/k12/assessment-guide-teacher-edition-grade-2/9780358104957 (E261)
5. **Report categories:** no public Math in Focus, EdReports or district text uses "concepts, skills
   and procedures" with "statements, arguments or justifications". The closest public wording is
   Smarter Balanced Claim 1 "Concepts and Procedures" and Claim 3 "Communicating Reasoning"
   (construct viable arguments; explain, justify), via state education department documents
   (search excerpts). *Confirmed (Smarter wording) · Medium; link to his cover sheet Speculative ·
   Low* — https://portal.ct.gov/-/media/sde/student-assessment/smarter-balanced-presentations/mathematics_claim_3.pdf ;
   https://doe.sd.gov/Assessment/documents/MATHCLAIM.pdf (E262; E133 unchanged)
6. **Points per item** remain unpublished for every Grade 1–3 test except his own paper (2/2/3).
   *Confirmed (absence in searched sources) · Medium* (E263)

**Verdict: PARTLY CLOSED** — the Grades 1–3 format picture is now complete enough to design
rehearsal surfaces (Grade 1 already has error analysis and shown work); point weights and the
cover-sheet categories stay open → design default (OPEN-GAPS-FOR-DEFAULTS.md, G5).

## G6 ★5 — Heuristics by grade

**Searches tried.** PM 2022 TOC "Solve! Heuristics" per chapter (vector 1); PM 2022 scope process
strand; Nutley Grade 1 and 3 guides for "Thinking Cap" (vector 2); EdReports Grades 1, 3, 5 for
heuristic names; the 2026 Singapore deck re-read (vector 4, CR-15); web search `Singapore primary
math heuristics taught by level P1 P2 P3 "act it out" "draw a diagram" "guess and check" "work
backwards"`; web search `site:isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg mathematics parents workshop heuristics
model drawing P3 P4`. Every `*.moe.edu.sg/files/` deck found (six schools) returned 404. Not run:
Dimensions per-book overviews and singaporemath.com pages (budget).

**Findings.**
1. **PM 2022 names one heuristic per chapter (publisher TOC):**
   Grade 1 — Make a List (Ch 4, 9), Guess and Check (Ch 10), Look for a Pattern (Ch 11).
   Grade 2 — Guess and Check (Ch 2), Work Backwards (Ch 3), Draw a Diagram (Ch 4, 5), Look for a
   Pattern (Ch 6), Make a List (Ch 9).
   Grade 3 — Look for a Pattern (Ch 3), Guess and Check (Ch 4), Make a Supposition (Ch 6), Act It
   Out (Ch 8), Make a List (Ch 9).
   Grade 4 — Use Before-After Concept (Ch 2), Tabulating (Ch 4), Simplify the Problem (Ch 6), Look
   for a Pattern (Ch 7), Make a Supposition (Ch 8), Guess and Check (Ch 10).
   Grade 5 — Restate the Problem (Ch 2), Look for a Pattern (Ch 4), Write an Equation (Ch 5), Use
   Before-After Concept (Ch 7), Make a Supposition (Ch 8), Guess and Check (Ch 9).
   The scope's Grades 3–5 columns add "Apply thinking skills in Think!, Math Talk, Heuristics".
   *Confirmed (titles) · High* —
   https://singapore-math.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/scopes/PM22_Scope_Overviews.pdf (E264)
2. **Math in Focus: no per-chapter heuristic is public.** EdReports Grades 1, 3 and 5 mention
   "Put On Your Thinking Cap!" only as one pacing day with Math Journal; no heuristic named. Nutley
   files Thinking Cap pages under Gifted and Talented enrichment in Grades 1 and 3.
   *Confirmed (absence / that district) · High* (E265, E266)
3. **Singapore level mapping is secondary only:** a commercial tuition guide maps P1–P2 to act it
   out, a simple diagram, patterns, guess and check, and P3–P4 to lists, tables, work backwards,
   before-after, restate; a school synopsis (snippet; path retired) describes a P3/P4 session on
   before-after and work backwards. *Speculative · Low* (E267)
4. **The 2026 Singapore deck (re-read):** "Get a plan — Heuristics" is one step of its R.I.G.H.T.
   routine, which also has a keyword step; think-aloud prompts confirmed. *Confirmed (that school)
   · High* (E248; CR-15 closed)

**Changes the earlier picture.** R2's working guess (E95: work backwards and supposition only at
P5–P6) is contradicted by PM 2022, which teaches **work backwards in Grade 2** and **supposition
from Grade 3**; before-after first appears in Grade 4 (CR-17). **Verdict: PARTLY CLOSED** — a
confirmed per-grade sequence exists for one current US Singapore-lineage series; the Singapore
ministry's and Math in Focus's own sequences are still not public.
