## G1 — The school's own Grade 2 calendar (OWNER item)

- **Still unknown:** the class's current chapter, the Ch 4 start date, the Book B order, the next
  test date.
- **Moves:** when the Grade 2 entry plan pre-teaches part-whole and comparison bars; when it
  rehearses Ch 3 formats; review timing.
- **Fixed by the ledger:** three public district calendars put Ch 3 around mid-November and Ch 4
  late November into January (E139); Book B order varies by district (E137–E141); Ch 4 sections
  run part-whole → adding on / taking away → comparison → two-step (E60); the Ch 4 test asks him
  to complete a bar model, the Ch 6 test to draw one (E129, E243).
- **Basis for the default:** pre-teaching a representation before the class meets it lowers load
  at first exposure (working memory, E63, E228); order-independence keeps the plan valid whatever
  Book B order the school uses; mixed and spaced review (IES Rec. 1, E90, E167).
- **Closes it:** the owner, from the teacher's pacing note, the School-to-Home letter at the front
  of the current Extra Practice chapter, assignment titles on the Ed login, or the next test date.

## G2 (residual) — Inside Grade 2: label supplied bars, or size them himself?

- **Still unknown:** whether Math in Focus Grade 2 lessons ask the child to label or to size bars
  (the "label" rung rests on one district, E62); the Teacher Edition's CPA-progression pages are
  teacher-only.
- **Moves:** the Grade 2 rung ladder on screen and what a Grade 2 bar item asks the child to do.
- **Fixed by the ledger:** Grade 1 has no bars (E246); Grade 2 lessons supply the model ("use the
  bar model", E243); the Ch 4 test asks him to complete a blank bar, the Ch 6 test to draw one
  (E61, E129, E243); Grade 3 lessons ask the pupil to draw (E244, E245); a Singapore P2 class asks
  the child which model to draw and where the values go (E248). So by the Grade 2 Ch 6 test he must
  be able to draw one unaided.
- **Basis for the default:** fading from supplied to child-built models (E59); the production
  deficit — children judge given diagrams better than they draw them (E64), so recognising is not
  enough; guided construction is the praised pattern and fill-the-blank is criticised (E219,
  E224); each picture must connect explicitly to a number sentence (IES Rec. 3, E167; E229).

## G6 (residual) — Singapore ministry and Math in Focus heuristic sequences

- **Still unknown:** the ministry's (or Math in Focus's) heuristic-by-grade sequence; which
  heuristic each Math in Focus "Put On Your Thinking Cap!" uses (E266).
- **Moves:** the heuristics strand, Grades 1–5, and the Thinking-Cap-style items.
- **Fixed by the ledger:** PM 2022's per-chapter sequence, Grades 1–5 (E264, E268): Grade 1 make a
  list, guess and check, look for a pattern; Grade 2 adds work backwards and draw a diagram; Grade 3
  supposition and act it out; Grade 4 before-after, tabulating, simplify; Grade 5 restate, write an
  equation. Ministry heuristic groups (E91); Math in Focus heuristics poster (E92); Thinking Cap is
  a non-routine, chapter-end item (E96) and one district treats it as enrichment (E265).
- **Basis for the default:** heuristics without monitoring fail (E97) — pair each heuristic with a
  check step; metacognition is a framework component (E98); no controlled study shows general
  heuristic transfer in Grades 1–3 (INVENTORY §3 item 11), so treat heuristics as named moves
  practised inside problems, not as a separate taught skill.

## G8 — Building a bar model on screen at age 7, and transfer to paper

- **Moves:** the core model interaction of the module.
- **Fixed by the ledger (new in Phase 3):** the handover ladder (E247): supplied in Grade 2
  lessons, completed on the Ch 4 test, drawn on the Ch 6 test, drawn in Grade 3 lessons; Singapore
  P2 prompts ask which model and where the values go (E248). No study compares screen and paper at
  Grades 1–3 (E179, E227).
- **Basis for the default:** fading (E59); production deficit (E64); guided construction praised,
  fill-the-blank criticised (E219, E224); connect each model to a number sentence (IES Rec. 3,
  strong, E167; E229); drawing mechanics cost working memory (E63, E228); virtual manipulatives
  show a moderate effect (E166); manipulatives help retention more than transfer (E164), so paper
  transfer needs its own practice.
