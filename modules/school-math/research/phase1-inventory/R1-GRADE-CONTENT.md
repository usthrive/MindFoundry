## 2. Singapore MOE primary mathematics syllabus (2021, current)

Source S1 (official PDF, Oct 2025 update; MOE grants permission to reproduce it for personal,
non-commercial use). Read in full on 2026-11-17. Facts below are structure and topic titles only.

### 2.1 Status and implementation — Confirmed · High (S1)
- Title page: "Implementation starting with 2021 Primary One cohort".
- Footnote on the content pages: in 2025 the 2021 syllabus applied to P1–P5 only; P6 stayed on
  the 2013 syllabus and moves to the 2021 syllabus "from 2026 onwards". So in 2026 (now) the
  2021 syllabus covers all of P1–P6.
- P1–P4 is common to all students. P5–P6 split into **Standard** and **Foundation** Mathematics
  (Foundation re-visits P1–P4 ideas; its new content is a subset of Standard).
- The syllabus "assumes no formal learning of mathematics" before P1.

### 2.2 Framework — Confirmed · High (S1, pp. 8–14)
- **Central focus: mathematical problem solving**, supported by five inter-related components
  (the "pentagon"): **Concepts · Skills · Processes · Metacognition · Attitudes**.
  - Concepts: numbers, algebra, measurement, geometry, statistics.
  - Skills: numerical calculation, algebraic manipulation, spatial visualisation, data analysis,
    measurement, use of tools, estimation.
  - Processes: abstracting, reasoning, representing and communicating, applying and modelling
    (true modelling is secondary; primary does real-world problems + reasonableness checks).
  - Metacognition: monitoring and regulating one's own thinking and strategy choice.
  - Attitudes: belief, appreciation, confidence, motivation, interest, perseverance.
  - Pólya's 4 steps and heuristics are named as general problem-solving strategies.
- **Four themes** (new in 2021): Properties and Relationships · Operations and Algorithms ·
  Representations and Communications · Abstractions and Applications.
- **Six clusters of big ideas** (new in 2021): Diagrams · Equivalence (incl. part-whole
  relationships) · Invariance · Measures · Notations · Proportionality.
- **Three key focus areas** of the revision: 21st-century-competency processes; awareness of big
  ideas; greater emphasis on metacognition.
- **Pedagogy** (S1 pp. 18–21): Singapore Teaching Practice; three phases of learning —
  Readiness, Engagement, Mastery; engagement through activity-based learning, inquiry-based
  learning, direct instruction; mastery through motivated practice, reflective review, extended
  learning. Relational over instrumental understanding (Skemp 1976) — "knowing the why, not
  just the what and how".
- **Absence finding (Confirmed · High):** the 2021 syllabus text never uses the words
  "concrete-pictorial-abstract", "bar model", "model drawing" or "number bond" (full-text
  search of S1). Only "part-whole relationships" (under Equivalence) and "diagrams" appear. The
  CPA / bar-model vocabulary lives in the textbooks and teacher guides (and the 2013 syllabus —
  see §3), not in the 2021 ministry content list.

### 2.3 Strands — Confirmed · High (S1 p. 30)
Three content strands: **Number and Algebra · Measurement and Geometry · Statistics**.
Sub-strands used at P1–P6: Whole Numbers, Fractions, Decimals, Percentage, Ratio, Rate, Algebra,
Money (all Number and Algebra); Measurement, Area and Volume, Geometry (Measurement and
Geometry); Data Representation and Interpretation, Data Analysis (Statistics).

### 2.4 Topic list per level (Standard) — Confirmed · High (S1 pp. 31–44)

**P1** — Number & Algebra: Numbers up to 100 (counting; place value tens/ones; numerals and
words; comparing sets; comparing/ordering; number patterns; ordinal numbers 1st–10th) ·
Addition and Subtraction (concepts; + – =; relationship between + and –; adding more than two
1-digit numbers; within 100; algorithms; mental calculation within 20, 2-digit ± ones without
renaming, 2-digit ± tens) · Multiplication and Division (concepts; ×; multiplying within 40;
dividing within 20) · Money (cents up to $1; dollars up to $100). Measurement & Geometry: Length
in cm (measure, compare, draw to nearest cm) · Time (to 5 minutes; am/pm; h and min; one
hour / half hour durations) · 2D shapes (rectangle, square, triangle, circle, half and quarter
circle; forming figures; copying on dot/square grid). Statistics: Picture graphs.

**P2** — Numbers up to 1000 (counting in tens/hundreds; place value H/T/O; numerals and words;
compare/order; patterns; odd and even) · Addition and Subtraction (algorithms up to 3 digits;
mental: 3-digit ± ones/tens/hundreds) · Multiplication and Division (tables of 2, 3, 4, 5 and
10; ÷; relationship × and ÷; within the tables; mental within those tables) · **Fractions**
(fraction of a whole; notation; compare/order unit and like fractions, denominators ≤ 12; add
and subtract like fractions within one whole) · Money (dollars and cents; decimal notation;
compare; convert to cents and back) · Length (m), mass (kg, g), volume of liquid (ℓ); compare
and order · Time (to the minute; hours and minutes; convert h/min ↔ min) · 2D patterns by
size/shape/colour/orientation · 3D shapes (cube, cuboid, cone, cylinder, sphere) · Picture
graphs with scales.

**P3** — Numbers up to 10 000 · Addition and Subtraction (algorithms up to 4 digits; mental: two
2-digit numbers) · Multiplication and Division (tables of 6, 7, 8, 9; division with remainder;
algorithms up to 3 digits by 1 digit; mental) · Fractions (equivalent fractions; simplest form;
compare/order unlike fractions ≤ 12; add/subtract two related fractions within one whole) ·
Money (add/subtract in decimal notation) · Length/mass/volume (km, ml; compound units;
conversions) · Time (seconds; start/finish/duration; 24-hour clock) · **Area and Perimeter**
(concepts; square units, cm², m²; perimeter of rectilinear figures, rectangle, square; area of
rectangle/square) · Angles (concept; right angle, greater/smaller) · Perpendicular and parallel
lines · Bar graphs (incl. different scales).

**P4** — Numbers up to 100 000 (rounding to 10/100/1000; ≈) · **Factors and Multiples** (common
factors, common multiples) · Four Operations (multiplication up to 4 digits × 1 digit and
3 digits × 2 digits; division up to 4 digits ÷ 1 digit) · Fractions (mixed numbers and improper
fractions; fraction of a set; add/subtract with ≤ 2 different denominators ≤ 12) · **Decimals
up to 3 places** (place value; compare/order; decimal ↔ fraction; rounding; add/subtract to
2 places; × and ÷ by a 1-digit whole number; whole ÷ whole with decimal quotient) · Area and
perimeter (missing dimension; composite rectilinear figures) · Angles (notation; measure in
degrees; draw) · Rectangle and square properties · Line symmetry · **Nets** (cube, cuboid,
cone, cylinder, prism, pyramid) · Tables, line graphs and **pie charts** (read/interpret).

**P5 (Standard)** — Numbers up to 10 million · Four operations (× ÷ by 10/100/1000; order of
operations; brackets) · Fractions (fraction as division; fraction → decimal; add/subtract mixed
numbers; fraction × whole; fraction × fraction; improper × improper; mixed × whole) · Decimals
(× ÷ by 10/100/1000; unit conversion in decimal form) · **Percentage** (part of whole as %;
discount, GST, annual interest) · **Rate** · Area of triangle (base, height; composite figures)
· Volume of cube and cuboid (unit cubes; cm³, m³; isometric drawing; liquid in a tank; ℓ ↔ cm³)
· Angles (on a line, at a point, vertically opposite) · Triangles (isosceles, equilateral,
right-angled; angle sum) · Parallelogram, rhombus, trapezium.

**P6 (Standard), briefly** — Fraction division (fraction ÷ whole; whole or fraction ÷ fraction) ·
Percentage (whole from part; increase/decrease) · **Ratio** · **Algebra** (letter for an unknown;
simple expressions; simplify; substitute; simple linear equations) · Circle (area,
circumference; semicircle, quarter circle; composites) · Volume (missing dimension) · Special
quadrilaterals (unknown angles) · **Average**.

P5/P6 Foundation (S1 pp. 45–50): re-teaches P3–P4 content (whole numbers, factors, fractions,
decimals, area, angles) plus a subset of Standard P5–P6 (rate, percentage, area of triangle,
volume, pie charts, average). Not used for the US grade matrix.

**Design reading:** the 2021 Singapore syllabus pulls measurement, time and data **earlier**
(P1–P4) and pushes ratio and average **later** (P6). The US-edition textbooks used in US schools
(Math in Focus 2020, Primary Mathematics 2022, Dimensions) follow Common Core, not either MOE
list, so the MOE list is a lineage reference, not the alignment spine.

### Summary of the timing gap (Inferred · High)
Singapore runs **about one year ahead of Common Core** in multiplication/division (P1–P2),
fractions (P2–P4), measurement units and conversions (P1–P3), decimals (P4) and
percent/rate/ratio (P5–P6). It runs **later** on rounding (P4 vs Gr 3), bar graphs (P3 vs Gr 2),
the coordinate plane (secondary vs Gr 5) and has no line plots or customary units. Math in Focus
2020 keeps much of the Singapore order (hence EdReports' focus scores); Primary Mathematics 2022
is re-sequenced closer to CCSS (no ÷ or tables in Gr 2, no mass in Gr 2, ratio/percent in Gr 6);
Dimensions keeps the classic Singapore order (×/÷ tables and fractions in Gr 2, ratio/rate/percent
in Gr 5).

## 10. What we found / what we could not find publicly

### What we found
1. The current Singapore MOE syllabus (2021, Oct 2025 update) in full from moe.gov.sg: problem
   solving at the centre of the five-part framework, 4 themes, 6 big-idea clusters, 3 strands,
   and the topic list for every level P1–P6 (Standard and Foundation). In 2026 it covers all of
   P1–P6. (Confirmed · High)
2. That ministry text does **not** prescribe CPA, bar models or number bonds by name — those live
   in the textbooks; Math in Focus's publisher says CPA is used at all grades and the bar model
   method is **first introduced in Grade 2**. (Confirmed · High)
3. Math in Focus 2020 chapter titles and order for K–5 from public district guides (Nutley NJ
   K–5; Madison NJ and Great Neck NY for Grade 2), cross-checked against EdReports' reviews of
   the "2021" listing (chapter counts 13 / 11 / 12 / 9 / 10 for Grades 1–5; 151–163 days).
   (Inferred · High; titles partly Confirmed by EdReports)
4. Grade 2 pacing by month from three district calendars: Ch 1 Sept · Ch 2 Oct (–Nov) · Ch 3
   Oct–Dec · Ch 4 (bar models) Nov–Jan; Book B order varies by district; Ch 6 Mass and Ch 9
   tables often moved or dropped. A class that tested Ch 2 on 27 Oct is most likely in **Ch 3
   Subtraction Within 1,000 in mid-November**, with **Ch 4 bar models next**. (Inferred · Medium)
5. Primary Mathematics 2022 and Dimensions Math full chapter lists Gr 1–5 from the publisher's
   public scope documents; Primary Mathematics Common Core edition unit list with day counts.
   (Confirmed · High)
6. CCSS Grades 1–5 domains, cluster headings and critical areas from the official PDF.
   (Confirmed · High)

   rounding, bar graphs and the coordinate plane. EdReports scores Math in Focus 2020 "Does Not

   Math® → Singapore Math Inc. (per HMH; US register shows joint owner with a Marshall Cavendish
