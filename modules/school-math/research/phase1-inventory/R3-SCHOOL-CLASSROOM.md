## 2. Lesson anatomy (2020 edition)

Main sources: the publisher's own **Math in Focus 2020 Program Guide** (HMH, print code dated
09/24; https://s3.amazonaws.com/prod-hmhco-vmg-craftcms-public/Math-in-Focus-2020-Program-Guide-2.pdf,
linked from https://www.hmhco.com/programs/sample-math-in-focus) [S1]; the **2020 Components and
Manipulative Kit Brochure** [S2]; HMH's **Research Evidence Base 2** page [S3]; EdReports' 2021-listing
reviews for Grades 1–4 [S5–S9]. The 2020 headings differ from the older 2010/2015 editions; the
older names that parents and blogs still use are listed at the end of this section.

### 2.1 The chapter, start to finish (2020) — Confirmed · High [S1 pp. 5–6; S5–S8]

The Program Guide prints the chapter as one flow. In order:

| Part | Where | What it is for (paraphrase) | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chapter Opener** + **Essential Question** | Student Edition, day 1 | States the chapter's big question tied to the learning objectives, "why" before "how" | Confirmed · High [S1 p. 5] |
| **Recall Prior Knowledge** + **Quick Check** | day 1 (EdReports: 1 day per chapter; Gr 4: 1–2 days) | Re-teaches the prerequisite skills; Quick Check is a **pre-chapter quiz on prerequisites** (Grade 1 up), in the book and auto-scored on Ed | Confirmed · High [S1; S2 p. 14; S3] |
| **Sections 1–X** (≈ 5 per chapter; each 1–5 days) | core | The lessons. Each holds the parts in 2.2 | Confirmed · High [S1 p. 5; S5–S8] |
| **Math Journal** | after the sections | Students write, in their own words, what they learned; metacognition | Confirmed · High [S1 p. 6; S3] |
| **Put On Your Thinking Cap!** | after Math Journal (Journal + Thinking Cap = 1 day per chapter) | **Non-routine** problems solved with the four-step model and heuristics; the publisher calls it an extended-response assessment | Confirmed · High [S1 p. 4, 6; S3; S5–S7] |
| **Chapter Wrap Up** | end | Summary of the chapter's concepts (content not public) | Confirmed (exists) · High [S1 p. 6] |

| **Performance Task** | end of chapter, Student Edition | Writing/product task scored with a rubric | Confirmed · High [S1; S3] |
| **STEAM Project Work** | some chapters only | Collaborative real-world task, rubric feedback | Confirmed · High [S1 p. 6 asterisk; S3] |
| **Benchmark Assessments** | mid-year, end-of-year, **Grade 3 up** | Cumulative; see §4 | Confirmed · High [S1 p. 6; S2; S3] |

EdReports' pacing for every grade gives the closing parts (Chapter Review, Performance Task, Project
Work, Chapter Assessment) 2 days per chapter together, and adds 4–8 days a year for Cumulative Reviews and
Benchmarks; Reteach, Extra Practice and Enrichment have **no days of their own** — they sit inside the
sections (Confirmed · High as EdReports' reading of the publisher's pacing; Gr 3
https://edreports.org/reports/detail/math-in-focus-singapore-math-2021/third-grade/gateway-one ; Gr 4
https://edreports.org/reports/detail/math-in-focus-singapore-math-2021/fourth-grade/gateway-one).

## 3. Representations by grade

### 3.1 What the publisher and kit say — Confirmed / Inferred as labelled

- Publisher list of hallmark models: **ten frames** (counting, adding, subtracting around 10),
  **number bonds** (number relationships, flexible facts), **number lines** (whole numbers, fractions,
  decimals), **place-value charts** (meaning of digits; support for the standard algorithms),
  **fraction models**, **bar models** (Confirmed · High, Foundations paper 2020,
  https://s3.amazonaws.com/prod-hmhco-vmg-craftcms-public/research/Math-In-Focus-2020-Foundations-Paper-LR.pdf [S4]).
- The publisher says the **bar model is first introduced in Grade 2** (R1 §4.4; Confirmed · High).
- **Manipulative kit by grade** (Classroom Kit table, [S2 p. 11]; columns rebuilt from the PDF's
  text positions, so **Inferred · Medium**): base-ten units, rods and hundred flats in **Grades 1–4**;
  thousand cubes **Grades 2–4**; **place-value chips only in Grades 4–5**; fraction circles and tiles
  **Grades 3–5**; coin sets Grades 1–4; paper money Grades 2 and 4; demo clock Grades 1 and 3;
  kilogram weight Grades 2–4; geoboards Grades 1, 3, 4; multi-colour counters, penny sets K.
  Place-value strips (1s–1,000s and 1s–1,000,000s) and student whiteboards / **place-value mats
  (Grades 1–5)** are also listed.
- So in the 2020 kit the **concrete regrouping tool in Grades 1–3 is base-ten blocks**, and
  **disks/chips arrive in Grade 4** — consistent with EdReports naming "place-value blocks" in Grade 2
  Section 2.5 and **place-value chips/disks** in Grade 4 Sections 1.1, 1.3, 2.1, 4.3, while Grade 3
  Section 5.3 puts **counters on a place-value chart** (Inferred · High; three sources agree).

### 3.3 How Grade 2 teaches regrouping (Chapters 2–3) — described, not copied

From the Great Neck "2nd Grade Math in Focus" objectives
(https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1715339661/greatneckk12nyus/ul2jhwdovxybfuj3zovh/grademifcurriculum_1.pdf)
[S11] and the Nutley Grade 2 Unit 1 rationale
(https://nutleyschools.org/sites/default/files/curriculum/NPS_Curr_Math%2CG2%2CAug2024_.pdf) [S12]:

- **Ch 2 Section 1** (add fluently within 100): extends the within-20 mental strategies to 100;
  pictures first, then the vertical algorithm; **number bonds used to add mentally within 100**.
  Inferred · High. (This is the lesson behind the child's Q1 "Add mentally" item.)
- **Ch 2 Section 2** (no regrouping, within 1,000): concrete materials; **counting tape** to count on
  by ones, tens, hundreds; then the vertical algorithm. Inferred · High.
- **Ch 2 Sections 3–5**: regrouping in ones, then tens, then ones and tens — each **with concrete
  manipulatives first** (base-ten blocks on a place-value chart, per Nutley), then the vertical
  algorithm; **number bonds to add ones and tens to 3-digit numbers mentally**. Inferred · High.
- **Ch 2 Section 6**: up to four 2-digit addends, "strategies and the algorithm". Inferred · High.
  (EdReports says this standard gets little practice — R1 §4.2.)
- **Ch 3** mirrors Ch 2: Section 1 number bonds to break numbers + counting tapes; Section 2 no
  regrouping; Sections 3–5 regrouping tens→ones, hundreds→tens, then both; Section 6 **across zeros,
  regrouping the hundreds first, then the tens** (Nutley wording). Mental strategies taught include
  **"add 10 and take away the extra ones" / "add 100 and take away the extra tens"** (compensation).
  Inferred · High.
- **Language of regrouping** a school uses with parents (school-authored workshop, 2018, older
  edition): pupils say the trade aloud — not enough ones; regroup a ten as ten ones; how many tens are
  left; the hundred becomes ten tens. Inferred · Medium
  (http://www.heritagechristian.info/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Workshop-Handout.pdf [S16]).
- Nutley's unit also uses the **number line for rounding to the nearest 10** to check the
  reasonableness of sums and differences. Inferred · High [S12].

### 3.4 How Chapter 4 introduces bar models — described, not copied

Great Neck objectives [S11], Nutley [S12], EdReports Gr 2 [S6]:

| Section | Model step | Label |
|---|---|---|
| 4.1 Part-whole in + and − | concrete sets of objects first, then a **one-bar part-whole model** for adding parts / finding a part | Inferred · High |
| 4.2 Adding on and taking away sets | concrete → pictorial; one-step real-world problems; pupils **construct a number sentence** from the picture | Inferred · High |
| 4.3 Comparing two sets | concrete comparison of two sets → **two-bar comparison model** for "how many more / fewer" | Inferred · High |
| 4.4 Real-world problems: two-step | bar models to represent two-step problems and write the number sentences; uses the Ch 2–3 methods | Inferred · High |

- **Who draws the bars?** On the Chapter 4 test the bar model is **printed blank** for the pupil to fill
  (EdReports cites Chapter Test 4 Item 5: two-step, numbers over 100, "a blank bar model and equation");
  Cumulative Review 1 Item 1 is multiple choice **with a bar diagram given** (Confirmed · High, [S6]).
- Nutley: pupils need not draw bar models independently until Grade 3; in Grade 2 they use paper
  strips and **label pictures of bar models that contain a "?"** (Inferred · Medium — district rule).
- EdReports (Grade 4 Gateway 2): pupils are usually **given** the model ("draw a bar model", bars
  pre-drawn) rather than choosing one; MP4/MP5 "not met" (Confirmed · High, [S9]).
- A school's parent workshop teaches a 7-step drawing routine (read, rewrite the question as an
  answer sentence, name the units, one bar for part-whole or a bar per person for comparing, chunk the
  story onto the bars, compute, answer and check) — **school-authored, not the publisher's**
  (Inferred · Medium, [S16]).

**Design reading (Inferred · High):** Grade 2 Math in Focus uses **base-ten blocks + place-value
chart** (not disks) for regrouping, **number bonds** for mental strategies, a **counting tape** (a
number-line-like strip) for counting on/back, and introduces **part-whole then comparison bar models
in Chapter 4**, with the bars usually supplied. The child's Q7 (sum and difference) needs a
comparison model the class meets only in Ch 4.3 — after the test he took.

### 4.3 Practice volume (what tests can assume was practised)

- Independent Practice sets are short: EdReports counts **16 problems** in each of the two Grade 3
  sections that carry 3.NBT.2 (fluent ± within 1,000) and calls that "not extensive"; for Grade 2
  four-addend sums EdReports points only to Section 2.6 Independent Practice (two problems cited) and
  judges 2.NBT.6 practice limited (Confirmed · High, [S6; S7]; R1 §4.2).
- Grade 4 Gateway 2: procedural fluency **met**; conceptual understanding only partly (limited
  chances to show it independently); application met; **MP1/2, MP4/5, MP7/8 not met** — problems
  heavily scaffolded, pupils told to solve "the same way", models supplied (Confirmed · High, [S9]).
- Grades 1–3 were never reviewed for rigor or usability (Gateway 2/3 "not eligible" because Gateway
  1 failed), so there is **no public independent judgement of practice volume or assessment quality
  for Grade 2** (Confirmed · High, absence; https://edreports.org/reports/detail/math-in-focus-singapore-math-2021/second-grade [S10]).

**Design reading (Inferred · High):** the chapter test is where the book's quiet items surface
(mental-strategy items, four addends, missing digits, error analysis, a Thinking-Cap-style word
problem). Practice in the book on several of these is thin, and Grades 1–2 have **no publisher
mid-year/end-of-year test** — so a parent sees a chapter-test score, never a benchmark, until Grade 3.

## 10. What we found / What we could not find publicly

### What we found
1. **The 2020 lesson anatomy, from the publisher's own Program Guide**: Chapter Opener + Essential
   Question → Recall Prior Knowledge + Quick Check → about five sections, each Think → one or more
   Engage–Learn–Try cycles → Hands-on / Math Sharing / Game / Let's Explore → Independent Practice →
   Math Journal → Put On Your Thinking Cap! → Chapter Wrap Up → Chapter Review → Chapter Test →
   Performance Task (+ STEAM in some chapters). "Let's Learn / Guided Practice / Let's Practice" are
   older-edition names. (Confirmed · High)
2. **Assessment layers**: Quick Check (pre-chapter), Try, Independent Practice, Chapter Review with
   state-test-style items, Chapter Test (sections A multiple choice / B short answer / C constructed
   response, rubrics for constructed response), Cumulative Reviews, Performance Tasks, and
   **Mid-Year/End-of-Year Benchmarks only from Grade 3**. Digital versions auto-scored on Ed, worded
   differently; some tests kept on paper to see the pupil's work. (Confirmed · High)
3. **The child's test is the publisher's Chapter Test 2**: EdReports cites the same Problem 4. So
   EdReports reviewed the child's print edition. (Confirmed · High)
4. **Representations**: base-ten blocks + place-value charts for regrouping in Grades 1–3;
   **place-value chips/disks arrive in Grade 4** (kit table and EdReports agree); number bonds for
   mental strategies (Gr 1–3); counting tape; bar models from Grade 2 Ch 4 (part-whole → adding
   on/taking away → comparison → two-step), usually **supplied** to the pupil; Grade 2 tests ask the
   pupil to complete a blank bar model (Ch 4) and to draw one (Ch 6). (Inferred · High / Confirmed as
   labelled)
5. **Grade 2 Ch 2–3 sequence**: fluency within 100 with number-bond mental strategies → no
   regrouping (counting tape, blocks) → regroup ones → tens → both, each concrete then vertical
   algorithm → four addends (Ch 2) / across zeros, hundreds first (Ch 3); compensation strategies
   ("add 10, take away the extra"). (Inferred · High)
6. **Home connection**: one School-to-Home letter per chapter inside Extra Practice and Homework
   (activities + key objectives, English/Spanish), Family Room and HMH Go on Ed, older per-grade
   parent videos now behind a 403; no free public publisher page explains the models to parents.
