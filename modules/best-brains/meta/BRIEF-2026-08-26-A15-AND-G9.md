WHAT IS MISSING IS NOT ONE THING, IT IS TWO LAYERS. This is the part earlier
costings got wrong, so it is spelled out.

 LAYER 1 — THE FIGURE PRIMITIVES. `lib/figures.ts` has thirteen figure types and
 EVERY ONE has a React renderer in `components/figures/*Fig.tsx` plus a
 dispatch arm in `BBFigureView.tsx`, alt-text in `figures/prompt.ts` and
 assertion support in `figures/assert.ts`. There is NO polygon, NO circle and NO
 net. `shapeFigure` is an alias for `angle-figure`, and `areaGrid` draws
 rectangles — neither can show a triangle, a parallelogram, a trapezoid, a
 circle or an unfolded box.

 That matters because the recipes' named misconceptions REQUIRE the picture:
 E18's is "uses the slant side as height", which cannot be posed unless the
 figure shows a slant side that is visibly not the height; E19's discrimination
 is "radius vs diameter", which needs both marked on one circle; E20's is "counts
 4 faces, not 6", which needs a net. A prose-only geometry week would be a
 different, worse week — and `bb-broken-promise-scan` exists precisely to catch
 content that promises a picture nothing draws.

 Per primitive: a params interface + a `BBFigure` union member in
 `figures/types.ts` · a factory in `lib/figures.ts` · a renderer `.tsx` · a
 `BBFigureView` arm · `prompt.ts` alt · `assert.ts` support · and coverage in
 `bb-figure-render-test` (50 figures × 3 sizes today) and `bb-animation-test`.

 LAYER 2 — `lib/geometry.ts` (G9), the generator family: core generators, a
 discrimination and an error-analysis per week, registered `AnswerDef`s and
 `VerifyDef`s, and the exact-arithmetic audit `compute.ts` demands. Watch three
 arithmetic traps: a triangle's ½·b·h is not an integer unless b·h is even
 (draw it even, or key a Frac — decide deliberately); E19 must DECLARE π ≈ 3.14
 and use the Dec ops, never a float; and the catalog asks E20 for prisms "incl.
 fractional edges", which is Frac work, not Dec.

 renderer work is the uncertain half — it is the first new figure type since the
 B1.0 renderer was built, so budget a spike before promising a number. Then E18,

 BEFORE BUILDING ANYTHING, RUN THE DESIGN CHALLENGE WITH FABLE on the primitive
