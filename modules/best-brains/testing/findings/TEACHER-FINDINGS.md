## Findings (ranked)

### T-1 — CRITICAL · Practice before teaching: Day-1 practice reachable before the first-encounter lesson
- **What:** On a fresh week (lesson never started), in-app history navigation to `/foundry/day/1/practice` renders the full practice page — item, keypad, hint drawer, Anchor tab — before Ms. Wren has taught anything. The persona severity ladder names "practice before teaching" CRITICAL, and R1's route-guard check asks exactly this question.
- **Where:** `frontend/src/modules/best-brains/session/weekLogic.ts:107-110` (`isDayActionable` derives tiles only from day sequencing — it never consults `isLessonComplete`, which sits unused ten lines above at `weekLogic.ts:18-20`) and `frontend/src/modules/best-brains/screens/PracticePage.tsx:110-112` (guard calls only `isDayActionable`). Day 1 is "today" whenever nothing is done, lesson or no lesson.
- **Evidence:** observation `r1_practice_before_lesson_inapp` (`practiceRendered=true`, url stayed on `/foundry/day/1/practice`); screenshot `screenshots/phase7/teacher/04-r1-day1-practice-inapp-probe.png` (verified a real practice screen: "DAY 1 · PAGE 1 OF 2", "What number comes just after 97?", "A little help?", "Anchor ⌗" — not a loading placeholder). Timeline proof: week PATCH to `in_week` happened 16:38:00Z; the probe rendered practice at 16:35.
- **Important nuance:** the direct-URL probe "bounced" (`r1_practice_before_lesson.practiceRendered=false`) — but that pass is almost certainly VACUOUS: on a cold load the `!weekState` hydration redirect at `PracticePage.tsx:106-109` fires before the guard is ever judged, sending the child to the hub. The guard itself has no lesson clause, so the deep-link "bounce" is a race, not a gate.
- **Pedagogical why:** the entire E41 Teach→Perceive→Practice→Perfect claim rests on practice being impossible before teaching. A child who back-navigates (or restores a tab) practices an untaught concept with hints that assume the lesson's language ("number path" is taught in the lesson's OUR WORDS).
- **Reproduction:** fresh week (enroll, no stage call) → land on hub → in-app `history.pushState`/client-side navigate to `/foundry/day/1/practice`.
- **Suggested fix:** in `isDayActionable` (or a wrapper used by `PracticePage`/`WarmUp`), require `isLessonComplete(dayProgress)` for every day route; keep the hub CTA behavior unchanged. One line of law, already written, just never wired to the guard.
