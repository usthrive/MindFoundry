## 3. What corporate provides to a franchisee (operating model)

- **Proprietary curriculum, centrally produced.** "Our non-repetitive, one concept at a time, proprietary curriculum promotes better understanding and retention" — franchisees do not create curriculum; teachers receive materials + answer keys. **Confirmed** (bestbrainsfranchise.com/about-franchise; bestbrains.com/careers). High.
- **Training:** ~34 h classroom + 10 h on-the-job (Entrepreneur); described elsewhere as two weeks at Arlington Heights HQ plus shadowing at an existing location. **Inferred**. Medium.
- **Marketing from Chicago HQ**, franchise success team, mentor network, year-round training. **Confirmed** (bestbrainsfranchise.com). Medium-High.
- **Technology:** bbConnect app (launched ~2020–2023) for online classes, homework on tablet + stylus, weekly graded feedback; monthly tech fee $40–$80. **Confirmed** (bestbrains.com/bb-connect) / fee **Inferred**. High/Medium.
- **Franchisee profile sought:** 4-year degree, passion for education, minimum ~25 h/week owner commitment; teaching background helpful but NOT required (the certified-teacher requirement lands on hired staff, not the owner). **Inferred** (1851franchise). Medium.

## 4. The delivered program (as described in franchise/owner-facing and corporate materials)

- Subjects: **Math, English, Abacus, Coding** (core), plus General Knowledge (legacy 4th subject), Public Speaking, Creative Writing, Test Prep/ACT-SAT, Summer Camp (K-8), Homework Help, Aftercare. Ages 3–14. **Confirmed** (bestbrainsfranchise.com, bestbrains.com). High.
- Class model: **once-a-week class per subject**, small groups, "no more than 5 students per teacher at a Math or English table"; ratio was cut to 3:1 during the pandemic per VP interview. Online bbConnect: **30 min live instruction/week/subject, max 3 students : 1 teacher**. **Confirmed**. High.
- Weekly learning cycle ("PEPP"): teacher **teaches the concept of the week** in class using the weekly homework packet → student **practices daily at home** ("10 minutes per subject per day") → understanding is "perfected" by week's end → next concept. **Confirmed** (bestbrains.com/article/what-is-best-brains, bestbrains.com/math). High.
- Feedback loop: weekly graded homework + **weekly personalized feedback to parents via a "Progress Book"** that parents must sign. **Confirmed**. High.
- Positioning: "non-repetitive" curriculum (explicit contrast with Kumon-style drill repetition); "real board-certified teachers" (contrast with Kumon/Mathnasium tutors); marketing claim "96% of families report higher grades within 6 months." **Confirmed** (marketing claims — not independently verified). High that they claim it; Low as fact.

## 6. What job ads and employee reviews reveal about the class/homework/grading operation

This is the clearest public window into the actual teaching operation.

### Teacher job postings (Indeed / ZipRecruiter / SimplyHired / vaia aggregations)
- **Role:** part-time certified teacher teaching Math & English **to groups**, students ages 3–14 (grades 1–8 typical). **Inferred** (many postings). High.
- **"Curriculum and materials will be provided for you and no class preparation is needed prior to each class"**; "No lesson planning is required; material and answer key will be provided." The teacher is a **deliverer of a scripted central curriculum**, not a designer. **Inferred** (repeated across postings) + **Confirmed** (bestbrains.com/careers: "No grading, lesson planning, or out-of-pocket expenses!" and "A proven curriculum designed by real teachers"). High.
- **Qualifications:** state-certified teacher OR currently in an accredited education program; tutoring/teaching experience in Math and English. **Inferred**. High.
- **Hours:** centers run limited after-school windows — typical posting: **Mon/Wed 4:30–7:30 PM + Sat 9 AM–12 PM ≈ 8–10+ h/week** (Austin posting); another center Tue/Thu 4:15–7:45 PM. This implies a center is open ~2 weekday evenings + Saturday morning, with all classes packed into ~3-hour blocks. **Inferred**. High.
- **Pay:** teachers $15–$30/h depending on market ($22–25 Austin, $25–30 elsewhere). **Inferred**. High.
- One aggregator posting (vaia, Austin) uses generic language ("plan engaging lessons") that contradicts the standard no-prep pitch — likely template boilerplate, not the real operation. **Speculative**. Low.

### Dedicated GRADER role — the homework engine
- Best Brains hires a **separate part-time "Math/English Grader"** position: "grade homework, with all answer keys provided," students pre-K–8 (one posting mentions high-schoolers). Pay ~**$10/h**; **11th–12th grade high-school students eligible**; hours match class nights (e.g., Tue/Thu 4:15–7:45 PM). **Inferred** (ZipRecruiter Pineville NC + SimplyHired Round Rock TX postings, Indeed grader reviews). High.
- Career page lists three roles: **Certified Teachers, Graders, Instructors** — confirming grading is structurally split from teaching. **Confirmed**. High.
- Operational read: the weekly homework packet is turned in at class, graded by low-wage graders against answer keys, and results feed the weekly Progress Book feedback. Teachers "focus on the tutoring" (one Indeed review: "grader was provided so I was able to focus on the tutoring"). **Inferred**. High.

### Employee reviews (Glassdoor ~3.9/5 across ~113 reviews; Indeed reviews)
- **Real class size in practice: 5–7 students per teacher, simultaneously at DIFFERENT grade levels**, working through leveled workbooks — "you guide students through math and English workbooks designed to meet them where they are." So a "class" is actually a multi-level small-group tutoring table, not a lecture. **Inferred**. High.
- Teachers must **write personalized feedback after each class** for each student (most students attend once a week). **Inferred** (Glassdoor). Medium-High.
- Pain points: managing 5+ kids on different lessons in a tight time slot is "overwhelming"/"challenging"; only 2–4 h/night of work available because of the narrow operating window; no holiday pay/PTO; some centers informally drop staff off the schedule (franchisee-level HR variance). **Inferred**. Medium-High.
- Positives: flexible, low-prep, materials supplied, good side income; work-life balance rated 4.4/5, pay 3.3/5. **Inferred**. Medium.

### Synthesis of the operating model (from staffing evidence)
**Speculative synthesis, Medium confidence:** A center is a lean 3-session/week operation (2 weekday evenings + Saturday AM). Staffing ≈ owner-director (25+ h/wk, sales/admin/parent relations) + a few part-time certified teachers ($15–30/h, ~8–10 h/wk each, zero prep, scripted curriculum) + $10/h graders + possibly an admin assistant (Entrepreneur's "5 employees minimum" fits). Revenue is monthly per-subject tuition, month-to-month, with royalty ~13–18% skimmed per subject. The pedagogical product = 1 weekly small-group concept lesson per subject + daily 10-min homework + externally graded packets + signed weekly Progress Book. Quality bar rests almost entirely on the central curriculum/answer keys and the "certified teacher" hiring filter, not on local instructional design.
