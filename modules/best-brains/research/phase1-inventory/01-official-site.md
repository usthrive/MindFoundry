Researcher: official-sources sub-agent. Date: 2026-07-19.
Method: WebFetch of live pages + site-restricted WebSearch (bestbrains.com only). All findings from official Best Brains web properties. No franchise-only or leaked material used.

Labels: **Confirmed** = stated on an official page fetched or surfaced via site-restricted search of bestbrains.com. **Inferred** = combined from multiple official pages. **Speculative** = plausible, unsupported.

- Ages: pre-K through high school overall (core programs marketed ages 3-14 elsewhere on site). — **Confirmed, High**

- Delivery model: 1-on-1 instruction with certified educators, "personalized academic plans", weekly teacher session + daily home practice, weekly feedback to families. — **Confirmed, High**
- Outcome stats claimed: "96% of families report higher grades within six months"; "90% report better performance than peers". Marketing survey claims, no methodology given. — **Confirmed (that they claim it), High; the stats themselves unverifiable**
- "360° development" framing (beyond academics). — **Confirmed, Medium**
- Nav structure: Programs, bbOnline (bbConnect, bbSupport), Why Us?, Resources (Blog, Videos, Tests by State), Locations, Franchise; CTA /new-registration. — **Confirmed, High**

## 2. https://bestbrains.com/math (Math program page)

- Three marketed tiers: Beginner ages 3-5, Intermediate ages 6-9, Advanced ages 10+. — **Confirmed, High**
- Topic span: number sense/counting, addition/subtraction, multiplication/division, decimals/fractions, probability/statistics, algebra, geometry. — **Confirmed, High**
- Core curriculum claim: BOTH "computational and non-computational" concepts covered weekly, designed to complement each other. This dual-strand weekly structure is a distinctive design element. — **Confirmed, High**
- Engagement strategies named: word problems, real-world applications, timed quizzes, puzzle solving — used "to keep daily practice fun and interesting". — **Confirmed, High**
- Progression: level-based; "concepts are taught one at a time", progress monitored, students move through levels at own pace. — **Confirmed, High**
- Teachers: "board-certified teachers", weekly instruction. — **Confirmed, High**
- New topic every week ("students explore new topics each week, keeping learning fresh, challenging, and engaging" — per site search snippet of this/related pages). — **Confirmed, High**
- Positioning: supplements/strengthens the child's regular school math; "any student can benefit". — **Confirmed, High**
- NOT on this page: pricing, class length, progress book details, sample worksheets, number of levels. — noted gap.

- Grading loop: homework "graded each week", performance/progress feedback weekly; homework can be REASSIGNED and RETAUGHT until concept mastered before moving on. — **Confirmed, High**
- Parents get behavioral/developmental insights, weekly reports. — **Confirmed, Medium**
- Curriculum parity claim: same personalized curriculum as in-center. — **Confirmed, High**
- Requires iPad/tablet with stylus support; iOS + Android apps. — **Confirmed, High**

## 6. https://bestbrains.com/article/what-is-best-brains ("What is Best Brains?" blog article)

What it is: official explainer article — richest single public description of the operating model.

Findings:
- Four-P weekly cycle (paraphrase): teacher TEACHES concept in weekly class → student PERCEIVES lesson via the weekly homework packet → PRACTICES at home in varied ways → PERFECTS by week's end. — **Confirmed, High**
- Class size rule: "a low student-to-teacher ratio, no more than 5 students per teacher", seated at tables (or online), preserving "1-on-1 interaction". — **Confirmed, High** (note: bbConnect page says max 4/class online — minor variance).
- Progress Book: parents receive "weekly feedback in your child's Progress Book" and must READ AND SIGN it. Physical signed feedback artifact = core parent-loop mechanic. — **Confirmed, High**
- Recommended daily practice dose: ~10 minutes per subject per day. — **Confirmed, High**
- Ages 3-14 for core; Abacus and Coding for 7+. — **Confirmed, High**
- Math curriculum = computational + non-computational strands (restated). — **Confirmed, High**
