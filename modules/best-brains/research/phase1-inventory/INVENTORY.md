## WHAT WE FOUND (usable evidence base)

1. **The weekly cycle, fully triangulated** (official + apps + jobs + reviews): one new concept per week per subject → 30-min small-group live class (in-center ≤5, online ≤3–4) by a certified teacher → daily 5–15 min homework from a weekly packet → single weekly submission → human grading against answer keys → **Passed/Review + %** verdict → Review triggers reassign/reteach (mastery gate) → weekly narrative feedback to parent (Progress Book, signed weekly + email).
2. **Explicit pedagogy brand**: "non-repetitive", "concept-based", "the why behind the math", Teach→Perceive→Practice→Perfect, anti-drill vs-Kumon positioning, Common Core / school-curriculum alignment (complement to school, not an independent ladder).
3. **Content architecture signals**: Level→Week addressing ("Level M1, Week 10"), worksheet unit codes, monthly printed booklets (~23–56 pages), dual weekly strands (computational + non-computational), four variety levers (word problems, real-world application, timed quizzes, puzzles), "Brain Buster" and error-analysis strands.
4. **Visual/worksheet structure** from 9 official samples + 2 official PDFs: low density, framed sections, directions banners, answer boxes, age-graded visual→symbolic progression, algebraic thinking threaded through all bands.
5. **Operating model**: teachers deliver a centrally-authored curriculum with zero lesson planning; grading is a separate role; placement is a free diagnostic decoupling level from grade; pacing is compressible (Summer Power Up); month-to-month pricing ~$159/subject.
6. **Digital state of the art (theirs)**: 5-app ecosystem digitizing a paper workflow — stylus handwriting on booklet replicas, daily page-unlock, zero gamification, thin read-only parent surface — with known pain points (sync fragility, turnover, answers-without-explanation, wait queues).

## WHAT APPEARS TO NOT EXIST PUBLICLY

1. The internal **level map**: number of math levels, level names/codes meaning, level↔grade correspondence, per-level scope-and-sequence.
2. **Placement test** content, format, length, or scoring; advancement criteria beyond "retaught before moving on"; the exact Pass/Review threshold.
3. **Progress Book internals**: page format, fields, comment structure (no photo/scan anywhere public).
4. In-center **class session length** (only online 30-min confirmed) and what actually happens minute-by-minute in class (no recorded class exists).
5. Weekly **packet internals**: pages per day, day-labeling, strand ordering, spiral-review presence (only the outside of booklets and 9 marketing pages visible).
6. Teacher **feedback-writing guidelines**, mistake-diagnosis method, or training content.
7. Any **independent efficacy data** (only the company's 2020 self-survey: 96%/90%).
8. Organic parent discourse on Reddit/major forums; genuine head-to-head parent comparisons vs Mathnasium/RSM/Eye Level.
9. Unit economics (Item 19), exact royalty mechanics, in-center fees.
10. Founder/method long-form explanation in accessible form (2 interviews exist but transcripts are blocked — retrievable manually if needed).
