# 03 — VIDEO evidence: Best Brains math program (YouTube + public video)

Research date: 2026-07-19. Method: full enumeration of the official YouTube channel via public page data (158 videos + 12 Shorts), YouTube-wide keyword search (8+ queries), watch-page metadata/description extraction for ~40 videos, public thumbnail-frame inspection for 15 videos, and download of the two official public worksheet PDFs linked from official video descriptions.

Labels: **Confirmed** = official Best Brains channel/material. **Inferred** = multiple independent non-official videos. **Speculative** = single-source or interpretation. Confidence High/Medium/Low.

Access limitations (documented, not for lack of trying): YouTube caption/transcript endpoints are blocked from this network (proof-of-origin token gating), so findings rest on titles, descriptions, thumbnails/frames, and linked documents — not full audio content. No video was watched end-to-end.

Disambiguation: "Best Brain" (one word "Brain") = unrelated Ghana exam-prep brand ("Best Brain Examination Consult", mock-exam videos by Study By Yourself EXAMS etc.) and "Best Brain International School" (Ghana). Excluded throughout.

## 2. Official public math worksheets recovered from video descriptions (Confirmed, High)

These are the only two Best Brains-branded worksheet artifacts published publicly by the company itself (as "follow along at home" handouts — explicitly public, not leaked):

**a) Addition/Subtraction Practice** — https://cdn.bestbrains.com/pdf/addition-and-subtraction-worksheet.pdf (2 pages)
- Layout: title banner with rule lines + bestbrains logo top-right; red "Directions:" strip; then FIVE problems per page, one per row.
- Each problem is a three-box row: [numeral + that many clip-art objects] + [numeral + objects] = [large empty box]. Child writes the total AND "draw a picture to match" — answer box deliberately oversized for drawing.
- Design signals: extremely low density (5 problems/page), number-with-quantity-picture pairing (subitizing support), dual numeric+pictorial response, friendly rounded sans typeface, one operation per page (page 1 addition, page 2 subtraction, same skeleton). Visual-first for the pre-K/K band.

**b) "Making Art with Math" handout** — https://cdn.bestbrains.com/pdf/Making-Art-with-Math-handout.pdf (2 pages)
- Page 1: four-color-theorem coloring activity — predict minimum colors for 4 line-art pictures (write count in box), then color under two rules (no same-color adjacency, fewest colors).
- Page 2: crossword with Word Bank (Intersect, Geometry, Five, Odd, Parity, Even, Theorem, Adjoining) — math VOCABULARY as first-class content ("parity", "theorem", "adjoining" for elementary kids).
- Design signal: this is the "non-computational" strand made visible — real math ideas (graph coloring, parity of regions from intersecting lines) wrapped in an art task + explicit vocabulary consolidation. Matches the site's "computational and non-computational concepts weekly" claim (Inferred link, High).

## 6. Cross-video synthesis — what the video record supports

1. **Math program identity (Confirmed, High):** school-complementary enrichment ("strengthen... performance in a student's regular math education"), weekly 30-min class + daily ~15-min homework from a monthly printed packet; non-repetitive weekly-new-concept messaging is the core differentiator claim vs Kumon-style repetition; certified-teacher staffing is the #1 marketing message across every era (2014→2026) and every uploader type.
2. **Materials model (Confirmed via convergent official + parent visuals, High):** pre-printed booklet packets distributed monthly (pigeonhole materials room, Ashburn), identical content digitized page-per-day in bbConnect (student walkthrough), ~15 min/day/subject, unlocked daily between weekly classes (official BB Connect description).
3. **bbConnect UX (Inferred, High):** tablet + stylus handwriting on digitized worksheet pages; page-counter pacing; pen/highlighter/eraser/ruler toolbar; no gamification visible anywhere in the record — it is a paper-workflow digitizer, not an adaptive app.
4. **Classroom form factor (Confirmed-franchisee, High):** small rooms, kidney table + ~6 child chairs (teacher at notch), brand-colored walls, whiteboard; small-group direct instruction, not classroom lecture and not 1:1 desk tutoring.
5. **Abacus strand (Confirmed, High):** distinct from math class; end-goal is mental visualization (air-fingering, serial multi-operand chains like 99−4−40−50−5+9); motivation via filmed weekly challenges and live competitions.
6. **Young-age math onboarding (Confirmed, High):** pictorial number+object worksheets, draw-the-answer response format, 5 items/page (the public Addition Practice PDF), Baby Genius+ (3–5) adds social/emotional readiness.
7. **Non-computational strand (Confirmed, Medium-High):** enrichment activities carrying real math concepts + explicit vocabulary (parity/theorem/adjoining crossword).
8. **Common Core alignment** stated by a franchisee ad (RTP) but never by corporate in video (Inferred, Medium).
9. **Pacing is compressible (Confirmed, High):** Summer Power Up = 6 months of the same packet sequence in one summer at 2–4 classes/week — curriculum is a linear packet ladder, not grade-locked.
10. **Video marketing maturity (Confirmed, High):** corporate video effort peaked as a COVID content library (2020–21) and has since devolved to 15-second localized ad spots; the brand does not use video to teach or to demonstrate the product.
